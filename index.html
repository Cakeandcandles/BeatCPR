Why CPR matters

An emergency can happen anywhere. Knowing what to do can make a difference before professional help arrives.

Too many people never get trained in CPR and basic life support—not because they don't care, but because the opportunity never came to them. BEAT CPR changes that by bringing free, high-quality training directly to you. Because accessible training is the difference between life and death.

What we teach

Classes are hands-on and shaped around your group, so participants leave prepared to step in and help. Choose the topics that fit, or ask us to cover all five.

  1. Adult CPR

    Recognizing an emergency, calling for help, and giving steady chest compressions to an adult.

  2. Child CPR

    How CPR changes for a child, from hand position to how hard and how deep to press.

  3. Infant CPR

    The gentler, specific technique used for babies.

  4. AED use

    How to use an automated external defibrillator by following its prompts, alongside CPR.

  5. Choking response

    What to do when an adult, child, or infant is choking.

How hosting a class works

Contacting us doesn’t commit you to a class. It starts a conversation about your group and how we can work together.

  1. 1

    Send an inquiry

    Tell us about your group, roughly how many people, and what you’d like to learn.

  2. 2

    We follow up

    BEAT CPR gets in touch to talk through your group’s needs, timing, and arrangements.

  3. 3

    We come to you

    An instructor leads the training at your location, with your group, in your space.

1–2 hours

Classes typically last around one to two hours, depending on group size and the material covered.

Free to host. Certification if you need it.
